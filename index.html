ABOUT BAKARI SAFARI

A new standard for Tanzania safari

Bakari Safari was founded on a simple belief: Tanzania's most extraordinary safari experiences deserve a thoughtful bridge to travelers who will cherish them.

We are a Canadian-incorporated tour operator specializing in bespoke private safaris in Tanzania, designed across a full spectrum of comfort and luxury, for discerning travelers from Canada, the United States, and Mainland China.

Our bilingual English/Mandarin team brings genuine cultural fluency across our three source markets.

We work directly with Tanzania's established lodge collections to deliver our journeys, with on-the-ground operations conducted through our TALA-licensed operating partner in Arusha. Every Bakari journey is designed bespoke, paced for intimacy, and supported by a team that knows Tanzania as both a place and a privilege.

A Spectrum of Journeys

From accessible introductions to bespoke ultra-luxury expeditions, our portfolio spans every level of safari experience.

Budget

CONSERVATION & COMMUNITY

Travel That Protects What It Celebrates

Tanzania's wilderness is not infinite. Every Bakari Safari journey is built around this understanding.

The lodges, communities, and wild places that make our journeys possible exist because of sustained, deliberate stewardship. We believe travel that takes from these places must also give back to them — not as marketing, but as operational reality. Every Bakari Safari journey contributes directly to conservation efforts and community initiatives in Tanzania.

Our Commitment

  • Direct support for Tanzania-based conservation efforts and community initiatives — taking forms appropriate to each partner, whether that is funding, infrastructure, capacity building, or long-term collaboration.
  • Active partnership with lodge operators and ground partners chosen for their genuine commitment to conservation and community impact.
  • Transparency in our impact — partner organizations, project focus, and meaningful outcomes published in our annual report once our commercial operations are established.
  • Long-term vision: the establishment of a dedicated Bakari Safari community and conservation program, with structure and partnerships to be formalized through our first year of operations.

Our partnerships are being thoughtfully developed in alignment with our journey portfolio. We believe meaningful impact is built deliberately, through sustained relationships, not announced prematurely.

STANDARDS

Our Operating Standards

How we work, on the ground.

  • Small group sizes — we cap private safaris well below industry norms for genuine intimacy.
  • Certified, experienced guides selected for both wildlife knowledge and cultural fluency.
  • Adherence to Tanzania National Parks regulations, community protocols, and lodge partner standards.
  • Conservation-conscious operations — we minimize single-use plastics, respect off-road and wildlife distance protocols, and continuously evaluate ways to reduce our environmental footprint.
  • Continuous review — operational standards revisited annually with our ground partners to incorporate evolving best practices.
